Beta 0.9.7

August 2026

🆕 New
  • 🎯 Avg 5×10 and Alpha 500 – the two classic speedsurfing scores, each with its own ranking and counting towards records and personal bests. Avg 5×10 is the average of your five best 10-second runs that do not overlap in time: a 2-second peak can be a GPS glitch, five separate runs have to be sailed. Alpha 500 is the fastest 500 m whose start and finish lie less than 50 m apart, so a jibe has to be inside it – a straight downwind blast does not count. Both are computed from your GPS track on our side, so every watch gets them without an update. Alpha depends on how finely your watch records: on a heavily thinned track it stays empty.
  • 🛰️ Satellite maps – track maps now open on aerial imagery, with a Map/Satellite switch top right and your choice remembered. On the water an aerial photo tells you far more than a street map: sandbanks, reefs, where the beach actually breaks.
  • 📊 Your figures inside the map – the headline speed sits in the picture, the session's values run along the bottom edge, and a colour scale explains the speed-coloured track line.
  • 🌍 Live page, no account needed – the latest sessions or the freshly broken spot records, with a map showing only the fastest stretch of a session, never the whole track. It is rendered on the server too, so search engines can read it.
  • 🖼️ Drawn sport pictograms – every sport has its own icon: in the page header, on the sport switcher, in Spot-TV and across the sport landing pages and home pages.
  • ⚖️ Sessions area in the backoffice – check a single session on a track map, release it or strike it, and cut a stretch out of it (the drive to the beach, a bridge crossing) while the original is kept and can be restored.
  • ⏱️ Active time – the watch now measures the time you were actually moving, to the second, and the session overview shows it next to the recorded time.
  • 🗺️ Three map modes in My Results – the whole track, only the fastest run, or only the longest run.
  • 📷 Spot pages show the live webcam and the spot photo, for visitors without an account too.
✨ Improved
  • Switch sport without leaving the page – Beat the Beach, Live and My Results each have their own sport selector, so you can compare sports on the page you are reading.
  • Beat the Beach shows the gap to the next place, not only to the spot record – further down the field the rider one place up is the target you can actually reach.
  • Fairer water check – it now looks for proof that you were on the water instead of counting how much of a track looks like land, and it samples the moving parts of a session. Sessions that start at the car (as Beat the Beach requires) are no longer punished for it.
  • Speeds are shown with two decimals – measured as precisely as before, just easier to read.
  • Weather can be fetched through the admin's browser when the weather service rate-limits our server, so missing weather can be filled in afterwards.
  • Surf is now on the guide, about and watch pages in all five languages, and has its own landing page.
  • The header row is one line of equal-height tiles instead of six tall buttons beside four flat ones.
🛠️ Fixed
  • Sessions wrongly excluded by the water check can be re-checked – including the ones stuck at exactly trust 30, which the first version of the re-check could not find.
  • Sessions whose watch sent no start position now take the position from the track, so they still get their weather and their spot.
  • Two bugs behind “no weather data” on freshly uploaded sessions.
  • Spot descriptions no longer carry copied header lines from their source.
  • The map's zoom buttons sat on top of the main figure and hid its first digits.
  • Clicking a sport from a subpage leads back to that sport's overview again.

Beta 0.9.6

🆕 New
  • 🎯 Beat the Beach – a page of its own for the record hunt: a ring showing how close you are to the spot record, your hunting list of all your spots sorted by where the least is missing, your progress at a spot over time against the record line, and what beating it would do to your rank. Beta 0.9

July 2026

📈 Session analysis (new)
  • Speed over time – every session now has a speed curve reconstructed from your GPS track, with the planing threshold, your runs shaded (longest highlighted) and your real top speed (GPS spikes filtered out).
  • Longest run – your longest continuous planing run is now recorded for watch sessions too (and we back-filled it for existing ones), so it counts in the rankings.
🔄 Gybe analysis (new)
  • Your manoeuvres, analysed. We detect your gybes & tacks and mark them on the speed curve with a traffic-light for how much speed you kept.
  • Pick a single gybe to break it into four phases – approach (−50 m), apex, rig-flip (+10 m) and exit (+50 m) – so you see exactly where a gybe drops off the plane.
  • Something no big tracker shows: not just that you rode, but how well your manoeuvres run. With the watch app update the phases are computed on the watch second-by-second for exact values.

Beta 0.8.4

Beta 0.8.3

July 2026

⌚ Garmin watch — much easier to get started
  • No more token copying: the watch app now shows a short connect code. Just enter it once on mywatersessions.com (Account → ⌚ Connect watch) to link your sessions to your account. It records from the first minute — nothing gets lost.
  • After every session a confirmation screen shows your upload and links straight to your ranking.
  • New app icon and a clearer recording indicator so you can always tell a session is running.
🗺️ Spot guide — "Who is this spot for?"
  • Spots now carry an orientation card: suitable level (beginner → pro), whether it's a good place to learn, water type (flat / chop / wave), typical wind and things to watch out for (offshore wind, current, rocks…), plus a per-sport matrix. Shown on the spot pages and in the app, with a clear safety disclaimer. It's guidance, not a guarantee.
  • Filter the spot list by "beginner-friendly" and "flat water".
🏆 On the site
  • Highest jump & airtime are now highlighted as ranking categories (wind sports & wakeboard).
  • Time for the distance: the 500 m and nautical-mile rankings now also show the time you needed for that distance – in the tables and directly in the Spot-TV bar.
🆘 Safety check-in (new, optional)
  • Set a personal safety timer and an emergency e-mail in your profile. When you start a session it can arm automatically; if you don't check back in (your next upload disarms it), your contact gets an e-mail. It's a convenience reminder, not a rescue service – no delivery guarantee, and it never replaces a buddy, leash or the coastguard.
📺 Spot-TV & performance
  • Spot-TV now pre-scales to fit a TV (adjustable) and shows the photo gallery in a single tidy row on big screens. The app also got a general speed-up.
