Connect your watch

MyWaterSessions works with any GPS watch. There are two ways in: some watches upload automatically, and every other watch or phone works by uploading a small FIT / GPX / TCX file. Here is the best way for each brand.

🌊 Open the app

Two mechanisms, depending on the brand:

  • Automatic – the session appears on its own, no file needed. This is Garmin and Polar: you connect your account once and we read your water sessions from there.
  • File upload – you export a FIT / GPX / TCX file from the watch's own app and upload it once. This covers COROS, Suunto, Apple Watch and everything else.

📍 Status — November 2026

  • Polar – automatic import: available now.

⌚ Garmin Automatic

Connect your Garmin account once – no app on the watch needed:

  1. In MyWaterSessions open ⌚ Connect watch · Garmin · Polar in the sidebar (right under My profile).
  2. Click 🔗 Connect Garmin, sign in at Garmin and confirm.
  3. Record with your watch's own windsurf, kite, SUP, surf or other water profile as usual. When the watch syncs to Garmin Connect, the session arrives in MyWaterSessions, placed at the nearest spot, and enters the ranking.

Only water sports are taken over – your runs and rides stay in Garmin. You can disconnect at any time, in MyWaterSessions or in your Garmin account.

Optional: the WaterSession watch app. The free app from the Garmin Connect IQ store adds live speed screens, the spot record as a ring on your wrist and values counted second by second (glide time, avg 5×10, gybes). Start it once, enter the connect code the watch shows under ⌚ Connect watch, and it sends every session straight to your account.

⌚ Polar Automatic

Polar has no app to install on the watch. Instead you connect your Polar Flow account once, and we import your sessions from there.

  1. Record and let your watch sync to Polar Flow as usual (via the Polar phone app or cable).
  2. In MyWaterSessions open ⌚ Connect watch · Garmin · Polar in the sidebar and click 🔗 Connect Polar.
  3. Sign in at Polar Flow and confirm. You are sent back to the app.
  4. Click 🔄 Sync Polar now. Your Polar sessions import and are placed at the nearest spot, so they count in the ranking straight away. Add board and sail under My Results if you like.

How does Polar know which watch? It doesn’t need to – the link is your account, not the watch. Any Polar watch tied to your Polar Flow account works; we simply read whatever sessions land in that account.

⌚ COROS File upload Direct sync applied for

  1. In the COROS phone app open the activity.
  2. Export it as a FIT (or GPX / TCX) file.
  3. In MyWaterSessions open Add session and upload the file.

We have applied for direct COROS sync – until it is approved, the file upload takes a few seconds.

⌚ Suunto File upload

  1. In the Suunto app open the activity.
  2. Export it as GPX (or FIT).
  3. Upload it in MyWaterSessions under Add session.

⌚ Fitbit File upload

  1. On fitbit.com open the exercise (recorded with GPS).
  2. Use the gear icon / Export → TCX.
  3. Upload it in MyWaterSessions under Add session.

Only exercises recorded with GPS carry a track and speed.

⌚ Apple Watch File upload

Via Apple Health – works with any GPS workout, even Apple's built-in Workout app:

  1. Record a GPS workout on the watch – the route is saved to Apple Health automatically.
  2. Open a Health-export app such as HealthFit or RunGap (they read your Apple Health workouts).
  3. Export the workout as GPX (or FIT / TCX).
  4. Upload it in MyWaterSessions under Add session.

HealthFit / RunGap can also auto-export every new workout, so you set it up just once. A watersports app that exports GPX directly works too.

⌚ Any other watch or phone File upload

If your device can produce a FIT, GPX or TCX file, it works. Export the activity and upload it under Add session – speed, GPS track, runs and every metric are computed for you.

Already on Strava or Garmin Connect? File upload

  1. Open the activity on Strava (Export GPX) or Garmin Connect (Export to FIT / GPX / TCX).
  2. Upload that file in MyWaterSessions under Add session.

Good to know

  • Sync to the cloud first. We can only see a session once it has reached the brand's app or cloud (e.g. Polar Flow, Garmin Connect) or been exported as a file.
  • File types: we read FIT, GPX and TCX – the common exports from every brand.
  • The spot finds itself. Uploaded and imported sessions are placed at the nearest known spot; if there is none, a new spot is created from the location.
  • One session, once. The same session won't be added to the ranking twice – not even when it arrives both from the watch app and from your Garmin account.
