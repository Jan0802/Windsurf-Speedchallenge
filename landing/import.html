Bring your whole history with you

Switching apps shouldn't cost you years of sessions. Import your history from Strava, Garmin Connect, GPS-Speedsurfing, GPSResults or any GPS app — in one go. Every session becomes part of your records and the per-spot ranking.

📥 Import in the app

Two ways in, and you can mix them:

  • Track files — drop many FIT / GPX / TCX / UBX / SBP files, or a whole ZIP, at once. We compute every metric (2 s, 30 s, 500 m, nautical mile, longest run) and draw the map.
  • Results CSV — only have the numbers? A CSV with date, spot and speeds brings your records in, even without a GPS track.

How it works — 4 steps

  1. Export your files from the other service (see the sources below).
  2. Open the app → My Results → 📥 Import your history.
  3. Drop your files or a ZIP. Each is matched to the nearest known spot by GPS.
  4. Set a default board (and sail) so imported sessions count in the ranking — you can edit any session afterwards. Done.

Where to export from

Strava FIT / GPX / TCX

  1. On strava.com go to Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account → Request your archive.
  2. You receive a ZIP with your original activity files.
  3. Drop that ZIP straight into the importer.

A single activity also exports via … → Export GPX.

Garmin Connect FIT / GPX / TCX

  1. Per activity: ⚙ → Export to FIT / GPX / TCX.
  2. Or request your full data export from your Garmin account.
  3. Upload the files (or the ZIP) in the importer.

GPS-Speedsurfing & KA72 GPX / SBP

  1. Download the track file you uploaded for each session (GPX, or the original .sbp).
  2. Most speedsurfers still have their originals in GPSResults — even better.
  3. Drop them (or a ZIP) into the importer.

GPSResults GPX or CSV

  1. Export your tracks as GPX and import them as files, or
  2. Export a results table (CSV) and use the “Results CSV” box — your 2 s / 500 m / nautical-mile records come in without tracks.

Old GPS loggers SBP / UBX

We read the raw logger files directly:

  • Locosys GT-31 and other SiRF units → .sbp
  • Locosys GW-52 / GW-60 and other u-blox units → .ubx

Or export GPX from GPSResults — either way works.

Any GPS watch or phone app FIT / GPX / TCX

If it can produce a FIT, GPX or TCX file, it works — COROS, Suunto, Apple Watch (via HealthFit), phone GPS apps and more. See also Connect your watch.

Good to know

  • No duplicates. The same session is never added to the ranking twice.
  • Fair & honest. Every imported track still runs through the GPS trust check that filters out implausible tracks — so imported records are as trustworthy as fresh ones.
  • Spot auto-detect. Each file is matched to the nearest known spot by GPS; pick a default spot as a fallback.
  • Set your gear. A default board (and sail) makes imported sessions count in the ranking; edit individual sessions any time under My Results.
  • What we can't import: services with no track export (e.g. Woo, Surfline) — there is no file to read.
